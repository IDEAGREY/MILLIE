Offline snapshot: IEEE Registration Authority public assignment lists, downloaded 2026-10-15.

Included registries: MA-L (24-bit), MA-M (28-bit), MA-S (36-bit), IAB (36-bit).
Source: https://standards.ieee.org/products-programs/regauth/
MAC address allocation overview: https://standards.ieee.org/products-programs/regauth/mac/

Lookup uses longest registered prefix. Locally administered addresses are excluded from organization attribution. The IEEE record identifies the organization assigned the block; it does not identify a product or prove a device type.
